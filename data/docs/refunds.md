# Refunds

## Refund window

A payment can be refunded in full or in part for up to 180 days after it was captured. After 180 days the refund endpoint returns the error code refund_window_expired.

## Partial refunds

A payment can receive several partial refunds as long as their total does not exceed the captured amount. Each partial refund must be at least 50 cents.

## Fees

The processing fee of 2.9 percent plus 30 cents is not returned when a payment is refunded. Refunds themselves are free.

## Timing

Refunds reach the customer's card in 5 to 10 business days. Refunds to bank debits take up to 14 business days.
